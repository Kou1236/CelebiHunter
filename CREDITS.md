# Credits

CelebiHunter is based on [PokeReader](https://github.com/zaksabeast/PokeReader) by zaksabeast and is distributed under the GPL-3.0-or-later license. It retains parts of PokeReader's Rust/3GX integration, English Crystal reader, Virtual Console hooks, and RNG infrastructure.

Documentation, research, and build tools:

- [Luma3DS](https://github.com/LumaTeam/Luma3DS), including the official 3GX plugin loader
- [CTRPluginFramework Blank Template](https://github.com/PabloMK7/CTRPluginFramework-BlankTemplate)
- [pret/pokecrystal](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal), for Pokémon Crystal disassembly and symbols
- [Pokémon RNG Guides](https://github.com/Admiral-Fish/PokemonRNGGuides), for community RNG research and terminology
- devkitPro, devkitARM, libctru, Rust, Cargo, and 3gxtool
